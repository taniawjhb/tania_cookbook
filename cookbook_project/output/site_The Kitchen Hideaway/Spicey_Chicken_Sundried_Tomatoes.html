Spicey Chicken & Sundried Tomatoes
1 tbsp. extra-virgin olive oil
5 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
2 cloves garlic, minced
1 tbsp. fresh thyme leaves
1 tsp. crushed red pepper flakes
1 tsp sicilian orgeano 
3/4 cup chicken broth 
1/2 cup heavy cream
1/2 cup chopped sun-dried tomatoes 
Kosher salt and black pepper
Fresh basil, for serving
Seasoned flour to dust chicken
Prepare seasoned flopur.. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, heat oil. Dust chicken lightly in flour. Sear until 
golden and skin is crispy, 3 minutes per side. Transfer chicken to a plate and pour off half the fat.
To skillet, add garlic, thyme, oregano and crushed red pepper flakes and let cook until fragrant, 1 minute. Add 
chicken broth, heavy cream, sun-dried tomatoes, and Parmesan and season with salt. Bring to a simmer, then 
return chicken to skillet and simmer covered, gently for 15 minutes, until chicken is fully cooked through.
Garnish with basil and serve.
