GORGONZOLA & PANCETTA

INGREDIENTS
240g short-cut pasta 
kosher salt and freshly-ground black pepper
½ cup Panko breadcrumbs
¼ cup plus 2 tablespoons Parmesan 
cheese, divided
½ cup crumbled Blue Cheese
2 cups shredded Fontina
1/2 cup half and half
2 cups whole milk
3 tablespoons all purpose flour
1 large leek (white and light green parts 
only), washed thoroughly and cut into thin 
slices
120g diced pancetta
5 tablespoons unsalted butter , divided, plus 
additional for buttering ramekins or baking 
dish
chopped parsley or snipped chives , for 
garnish
INSTRUCTIONS
Step 1:  Preheat oven to 200C with rack in middle position. 
Butter a baking dish, or six. Place on a baking sheet and set 
aside.
Step 2:  Mix Panko, 1 tablespoon melted butter, 2 tablespoons 
Parmesan, and a pinch of salt and pepper in a small bowl. 
Set aside.
Step 3:  Cook pasta to “al-dente” in a large pot of salted 
boiling water. Reserve 1 cup of the pasta water before 
draining well.
Step 4:  While bringing your pasta water to a boil, melt 4 
tablespoons of butter in a large, heavy-bottomed pot until 
foaming subsides. Add pancetta and cook over medium-high 
heat until fat is rendered and pancetta is just beginning to 
crisp.
Step 5:  Add leeks, ½ teaspoon each kosher salt and black 
pepper to the pot. Cook until leeks are softened, about 5 
minutes. Reduce heat and continue cooking, stirring often, 
until leeks are very soft and caramelized, about 15 minutes 
longer. Stir in flour and cook for 1 minute. Add milk and cream, 
stirring until sauce is smooth and thickened, about 3 minutes. 
Whisk in Fontina, Gorgonzola, and ¼ cup Parmesan. Season 
to taste with additional salt and pepper.
Step 6:  Add cooked pasta to the cheese sauce, stirring until 
well combined. Stir in enough pasta water to make the sauce 
creamy, but not thin and watery (I usually add about ¼ cup). 
Transfer pasta to prepared baking dishes and top with 
breadcrumb mixture.
Step 7:  Bake 15-20 minutes, until sauce is bubbly and 
breadcrumbs are toasted. Sprinkle with parsley or chives, if 
desired, and serve hot.
