Kisir - Turkish Bulgur Salad
INGREDIENTS
2 cups fine or extra fine bulgur
2 cups boiling water, plus more if needed
3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil, plus more 
for drizzling
1/4 cup tomato paste
3 tablespoons Turkish red pepper paste
1 teaspoon Urfa pepper or red pepper flakes
1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
Kosher salt
2 tablespoons pomegranate molasses
1/2 lemon, juiced
6 green onions
3 ripe but firm Roma tomatoes
1 cup chopped fresh parsley, plus more for 
garnish
METHOD
1. Prepare the bulgur: Place ½ cup of 
bulgur in a mixing bowl. Pour in 1 cup of 
boiling water, stirring gently to help 
absorption. Add just enough additional boiling 
water to cover the grains, then cover the bowl 
and let it sit until the bulgur is tender and has 
absorbed all liquid—about 15 minutes.  
2. Make the flavorful paste: Heat a small 
pan over medium heat and warm olive oil with 
tomato paste, Turkish pepper paste, Urfa 
Biber, cumin, and a generous pinch of salt. 
Stir until the pastes are fully melted and 
blended into a fragrant mixture.  
3. Combine the flavors: Pour the warmed 
paste mixture over the bulgur, then drizzle in 
pomegranate molasses, fresh lemon juice, 
and a little more olive oil. Mix thoroughly—
traditionally, this step is done by kneading with 
gloved hands to fully coat the bulgur in the 
paste.  
4. Cool and prep: Transfer the bulgur mixture 
to the refrigerator to cool while you prepare the 
remaining ingredients.  
5. Chop the vegetables and herbs: Trim and 
finely chop green onions (both white and 
green parts). Dice ripe tomatoes, ensuring 
they aren't overly watery—if needed, halve 
them and squeeze out the seeds before 
chopping.  
6. Assemble the salad: Once the bulgur is 
fully cooled, fold in the chopped green 
onions, tomatoes, and fresh parsley**. 
Taste and adjust seasoning as needed.  
7. Finish and serve: Garnish generously with 
more fresh parsley and serve immediately.
