EZME - THE DIP ALL SAUCE
INGREDIENTS
3 large tomatoes, firm and fresh
1 medium white onion
1 green bell pepper
1 red bell pepper
2 cloves garlic, minced
1/2 cup fresh parsley, finely chopped
1 tbsp tomato paste
1 lemon, juice of
2 tbsp olive oil , extra virgin
1 tsp kosher salt
1/2 tsp black pepper
1/2 tsp Aleppo pepper
1 tsp sumac
METHOD
1. Finely dice the tomatoes and place them in a 
bowl. Mince the onions and peppers just as finely, 
then fold them into the tomatoes.  
2. Stir in minced garlic and chopped parsley, 
ensuring the flavors blend evenly.  
3. Add tomato paste, olive oil, lemon juice, salt, 
black pepper, Aleppo pepper, and sumac, 
mixing thoroughly until every bite is infused with 
richness.  
4. Cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes to allow the 
flavors to meld.  
5. Serve chilled as a mezze, sauce, or vibrant 
salad alongside your favorite dishes.
