SHORBAT ADAS
CREAMY LENTIL SOUP
INGREDIENTS
1.5 cups Red Lentils
6 cups chicken/vegetable stock
1 cup Carrots chopped
1 Onion chopped
2 Garlic cloves chopped
1 tbsp Olive oil
Salt & Pepper to taste
1 tsp Cumin
1/2 tsp Turmeric
METHOD
1. In a pot, add the olive oil. To that, add the onions, 
garlic, and carrots. Add salt and pepper to this and 
then saute for 5 minutes until fragrant.
2.  To that, add the lentils and saute for a few 
minutes. Season with the cumin and turmeric. Mix 
together.
3.  Add the cooking liquid and bring this mixture to a 
boil. Once it reaches a boil, bring down to a simmer. 
Let this cook for about 25 minutes until the carrots 
are tender.
4.  Once tender, use an immersion blender to puree 
this soup until creamy. If you do not have an 
immersion blender, then pour the contents of the 
soup into a regular blender for a minute until 
smooth.
5.  Enjoy with crispy pita chips and fresh lemon 
juice!
