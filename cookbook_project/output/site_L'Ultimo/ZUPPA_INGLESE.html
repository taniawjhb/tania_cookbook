ZUPPA INGLESE
INGREDIENTS
710mls whole milk
2 tsp vanilla extract
1 strip lemon peel
6 egg yolks
150gms granulated white sugar
45gms all purpose flour
 60 grams dark chocolate, chopped
Assemble Zuppa Inglese
90mls Amaretto 
2tbsp water
18 savoiardi cookies or Madeira cake
200ml cream cold - (Optional)
100gms flaked almonds, toasted till 
lightly golden.
INSTRUCTIONS
Step 1:  Place the vanilla, milk, and a strip of lemon rind 
in a medium sized pot.  Heat over low heat until the milk 
is just before simmering point. Don’t boil.
Step 2:  In a bowl whisk egg yolks, granulated white 
sugar and all purpose flour until smooth. Take care that 
there are no lumps.  Remove lemon rind from the milk.
Step 3:  Slowly pour the hot milk onto the egg yolk 
mixture, whisking constantly.  Strain the mixture back to 
the pot and place over medium low heat. Whisk 
constantly until the mixture comes to a boil and thickens. 
Simmer for 1 minute to cook the flour.
Step 4:  Divide the pastry cream between two heat proof 
bowls. Cover one bowl with plastic wrap ensuring the 
plastic is in complete contact with the pastry cream. Add 
the chopped chocolate to the second bowl and stir until 
the chocolate has melted and is thoroughly combined. 
Cover with plastic wrap ensuring the plastic is in complete 
contact with the chocolate pastry cream
Refrigerate until thoroughly chilled.  Before using, stir until 
smooth.
Assemble Zuppa Inglese
Step 1:  Combine the amaretto with the water in a 
medium bowl.  Cut the savoiardi in half and dip quickly 
but completely into the amaretto mixture.  If using cake 
slice 1cm thick and line your serving dish, now brush 
generously with the amaretto mixture.
Step 2:  Layer a third of your cake or biscuits at the 
bottom of the serving bowl. Top with your chocolate 
pastry cream followed by a second layer of soaked 
biscuits or cake, then your vanilla pastry cream and a 
final layer of soaked cake / biscuits . Cover with plastic 
wrap and refrigerate for at least 2 hours to set. - Optioanl 
- Serve topped with slightly sweeteened whipped cream 
and toasted flaked almonds
