CROSTINI WITH ARTICHOKE

The Colony Shopping Centre, 345 Jan Smuts Avenue, Craighall, 2024.
CROSTINI WITH ARTICHOKE 
& PECORINO
INGREDIENTS
140g artichoke in olive oil, drained
pinch of chilli flakes
small handful mint leaves
zest ½ lemon
1 tbsp olive oil, plus extra for drizzling
2 large or 4 small slices of crusty bread
1 garlic clove sliced
50g pecorino thinly sliced (or vegetarian 
alternative)
INSTRUCTIONS
Step 1:  Heat oven to 200C/180C fan. Halve or quarter 
the artichokes, if whole, and put them in a bowl with the 
chilli flakes, mint, zest and oil. Season lightly with salt and 
set aside.
Step 2:  Put the bread on a baking sheet and scatter over 
the garlic. Drizzle with olive oil and season with sea salt. 
Bake for 10 mins, or until golden and crisp. Set aside to 
cool.
Step 3:  Fold the pecorino through the marinated 
artichokes and spoon over the garlic bruschetta just 
before eating.
