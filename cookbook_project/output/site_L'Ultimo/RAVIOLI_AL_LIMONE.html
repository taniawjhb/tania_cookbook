The Colony Shopping Centre, 345 Jan Smuts Avenue, Craighall, 2024.
RAVIOLI AL LIMONE
INGREDIENTS
For the filling
1.5 lemons, finely zested
500 g ricotta
60 g grated parmesan cheese
fine salt, to taste
Pepper, to taste
1 sprig thyme
For the Sauce
60gms butter
1 clove garlic, finely chopped
1 tsp finely chopped thyme
200 ml fresh cream
Zest of 1/2 lemon
2 tbsp lemon juice
INSTRUCTIONS
Step 1:  Place the ricotta in a bowl and season it with the 
grated zest of 1.5 lemons.  Add the grated cheese season 
with salt and pepper, add a little thyme, and mix well.
Step 2:  Take your pasta dough and run it through your 
pasta roller a few times, turning and folding each time to 
laminate the dough.  Now reun it through the machine to 
a 1-2mm thick level about a Level 5
Step 3:  Take a sheet of pasta and gently fold in half 
length ways to mark the centre point, then unfold again.  
Now place a generous spoonful of filling in the center of 
the bottom half and space before repeating, continue until 
all the ravioli are formed.
Step 4:  Wet the pasta around the filling, and now genlty 
fold over the top half, sealing from the inside out, press 
the pasta closed.  Cut out the ravioli, with a crfimped 
cutter.  Using a fork, seal the edges of each ravioli well 
and arrange them on a floured tray.
Step 5:  Salt a large pot of water and bring to a rilling boil, 
now add the ravioli in batches cooking for about 3-
minutes per batch.  Drain them directly into the pan and 
mix
Step 5:   To prepare the sauce start by browning your 
butter, once the butter is browned add the thyme and 
garlic and cook for 30-seconds, now add the cream and 
reduce slightly before adding the lemon zest and the 
cooked pasta.  Once the pasta has been added, take 2 
tbsp lemon juice and dilute in 50ml pasta water and stir 
through the cream sauce, top with freshly chopped 
parsely and serve immediately
