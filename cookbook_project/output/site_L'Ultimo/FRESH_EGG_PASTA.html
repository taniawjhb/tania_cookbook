FRESH EGG PASTA

The Colony Shopping Centre, 345 Jan Smuts Avenue, Craighall, 2024.
FRESH EGG PASTA
INGREDIENTS
400g 00” Flour
220gms of egg (About 4 eggs) 
30ml Olive Oil
Pinch of salt
INSTRUCTIONS
Step 1:   Put the flour on a work surface and make a 
well in the center.  Break the eggs into the well, adding 
your pinch of salt and the olive oil. Using a fork, start 
whisking the eggs and oil together, incorporating a little 
flour as you do. Slowly blend the flour and eggs, working 
from the center outwards
Step 2:   Knead the dough on a lightly floured surface 
for 6 minutes, or until dough is smooth, soft, elastic but 
dry to the touch. If sticky, add a little more flour. Once 
correct texture is reached, cover in plastic wrap and allow 
to rest for 30 minutes, before rolling out.
