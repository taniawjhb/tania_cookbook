TORTA DI MELE DELLA

The Colony Shopping Centre, 345 Jan Smuts Avenue, Craighall, 2024.
TORTA DI MELE DELLA 
NONNNA
INGREDIENTS
770g Red Delicious apples
3 eggs at room temperature 
290g 00 flour 
100g  butter
50g potato starch
150 g sugar
Cinnamon powder to taste
100g whole milk at room temperature 
16g baking powder
1 tsp vanilla
Juice of ½ lemon
INSTRUCTIONS
Step 1:  To prepare grandma's apple pie, first peel the 
apples and remove the core.  Cut them into thin slices. 
Place them in a bowl with the juice of half a lemon. Mix 
to distribute the juice better and prevent them from 
blackening.
Step 2:  Melt the butter, cut into cubes, in the microwave 
for 30 seconds, or in a saucepan over very low heat. Let 
it cool. While it is cooling combine the flour, baking 
powder, corn flour and cinnamon in a bowl and whisk to 
combine.  Add the eggs and sugar in a bowl and whisk 
until well combined - about two minutes, then add the 
melted butter and whisk to combine.  
Step 3:  Now add half the dry ingredients and whisk until 
just combined followed by half the milk, repeat until 
ingredients are finished
4:  When it's ready, add two thirds of the apples to the 
batter mixture and stir gently until well combined. 
Step 5:  Pour the mixture into a greased and floured 
cake tin, preferably a 22cm diameter springform cake tin.  
Then level it and sprinkle the remaining apples on top. 
Step 6:  Bake in a preheated oven at 180°C for 55 
minutes. Test with a toothpick to see if it's cooked 
through before removing from the oven. Let the apple pie 
cool completely before unmolding and serving.
