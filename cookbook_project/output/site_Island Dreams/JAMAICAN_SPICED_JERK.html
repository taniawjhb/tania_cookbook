JAMAICAN SPICED JERK

1 medium onion, coarsely chopped 3 
medium scallions, chopped
2 Scotch bonnet chiles, chopped 2 
garlic cloves, chopped
1 tablespoon five-spice powder
1 tablespoon allspice berries, coarsely ground
1 tablespoon coarsely ground pepper
1 teaspoon dried thyme, crumbled
1 teaspoon freshly grated nutmeg
1 teaspoon salt
1/2 cup soy sauce
1 tablespoon vegetable oil
Two 1,5kg chickens, quartered
In a food processor, combine the onion, scallions, chiles, garlic, five- 
spice powder, allspice, pepper, thyme, nutmeg and salt; process to 
a coarse paste. With the machine on, add the the soy sauce and oil 
in a steady stream. Pour the marinade into a large, shallow dish, 
add the chicken and turn to coat. Cover and refrigerate overnight. 
Bring the chicken to room temperature before proceeding.
Light a grill. Grill the chicken over a medium-hot fire, turning 
occasionally, until well browned and cooked through, 35 to 40 
minutes. (Cover the grill for a smokier flavor.) Transfer the chicken to 
a platter and serve.
