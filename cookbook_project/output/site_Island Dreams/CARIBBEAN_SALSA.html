CARIBBEAN SALSA

2 cups fresh diced tomatoes (or canned when 
out of season)
1 onion finely chopped
1 teaspoon chopped garlic
1 tablespoon olive oil
1/2 teaspoon cumin, chili powder, curcuma, 
paprika. (each)
1 table spoon orange juice (or lime) 
dash of honey
1/2 cup diced mango (or pineapple) 
salt to taste
In a small pan warm the oil with spices and garlic over medium 
heat
Once fragrant , add onions, cook until just clear
Now add all other ingredients let simmer until salsa is thick 
enough, taste and adjust with a little salt.
