Mexican Chipotle Mussels
1 kg fresh mussels
1 medium onion, finely chopped
1 clove garlic, minced
2 tomatoes, chopped
1 jalapeno, deseeded and chopped (optional) 
1/2 tbsp. olive oil
1 tsp. chipotle paste
1 x 330ml bottles of Corona or Sol beer
zest and juice of 1 or 2 limes, plus extra wedges to serve 
crusty bread, to serve
Heat 1tbsp oil and add the onions and fry until just softened, now add the chopped jalapeno and garlic, cook for 
2 minutes until fragrant
Now add the chipotle paste and the chopped tomato and cook for 1-2 minutes.
Now add the beer and bring to a simmer, cook for 2 minutes before adding the mussels and covering with a 
tight fitting lid.
Stir in the lime zest and juice and check the seasoning. Divide among 4 soup bowls and serve with extra lime 
wedges and crusty bread to mop up the juices.
