CRÈME ANGLAISE
1160ml full cream milk
4 tsp pure vanilla extract with seeds
8 large free-range egg yolks
6 tbsp caster sugar
Put the milk in a medium saucepan with the vanilla pod and seeds and slowly bring just up to the boil. Set aside
to infuse for 10 minutes. Remove the pod.
In a mixing bowl, beat the egg yolks with the sugar using a wooden spoon until well combined. Pour in the
infused milk, stirring constantly until well combined.
Return the custard to the cleaned pan and heat gently, stirring, for about 5 minutes until it thickens and lightly
coats the back of a spoon. Don’t let it boil or the yolks will scramble (see tips).
When the custard has reached the desired thickness, remove it from the heat and strain through a sieve into a
medium bowl. Put a piece of cling film on the surface to prevent a skin forming and set aside or chill until ready
to use.
