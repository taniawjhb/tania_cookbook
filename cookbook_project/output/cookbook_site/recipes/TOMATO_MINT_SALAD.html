TOMATO & MINT SALAD


Ingredients


400g cherry tomato
1small red onion
handful of mint leaves
1/2 tsp finely chopped thyme leaves
extra-virgin olive oil, for drizzling
a generous pinch of lemon zest
INSTRUCTIONS
Step 1: In a bowl add the chopped tomatoes, torn mint
leaves and thyme leaves, the finely chopped onion and a
generous pinch of lemon zest. Add the olive oil and then
season with salt and pepper, before tossing to coat, set
aside. When ready serve on a platter with all the juices
to mop up with crusty bread.
