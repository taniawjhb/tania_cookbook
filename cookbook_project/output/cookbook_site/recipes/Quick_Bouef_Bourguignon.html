Quick Bouef Bourguignon
900 gms chuck steak, cut into 2-3cm pieces
1 cup dry red wine
1/2 cup beef stock
2 carrots, sliced 1/2 cm thick
3 slices of bacon
225 gms fresh mushrooms, quartered
2 tbs. flour
12 small pearl onions or 1 large onion, chopped
1/4 tsp. basil
2 cloves garlic, minced
Slice bacon into 1 cm pieces and fry in cooker. Add onion and cook for 2 minutes.
Add steak and brown meat for 5 minutes be careful to not over crowd the pressure cooker.
Stir in flour and blend thoroughly. Stir in wine, beef stock and seasonings. Close the lid.
Cook for 25 minutes. Remove the lid.
Add carrots and mushrooms and simmer with the lid until done, about 15-20 minutes, if needed add a little stock if the
gravy is too thick
