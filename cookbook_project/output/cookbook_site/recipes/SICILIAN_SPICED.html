SICILIAN SPICED

From: Mangiamo - A Collaboration..pdf

← Back to Index | Ingredient Index

Ingredients


450gms kingklip / tuna fillet
2 large juicey
lemons
1/2 large tomato
1 shallot
1 large bunch of fresh mint
1 handful of basil leaves
1 pinch of cinnamon powder
1 teaspoon of extra virgin olive oil. salt


Method


1. Squeeze the lemons and reserve the juice in a bowl.
Cut the kingklip fillet into small cubes, place it in a bowl
and cover it with lemon juice. Keep it aside and leave it
to marinate, at room temperature, for at least a couple
of hours.
2. Meanwhile finely chop the shallot, tomato and fresh
herbs. Put everything in a bowl, season with olive oil, a
pinch of salt and cinnamon. Keep it aside.
3. When the fish is ready (the meat is opaque), drain
and gently combine the contents of the two bowls.
4. Serve with crusty bread
