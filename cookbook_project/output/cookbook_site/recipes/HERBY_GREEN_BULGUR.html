HERBY GREEN BULGUR

From: Sunshine on a Plate.pdf

← Back to Index | Ingredient Index

2 cups Bulgur Wheat dry (I used Turkish
Bulgur)
3 cups boiling water
1 tsp. Salt or more to taste
1/4 cup olive oil
1 cup cucumber diced
1 Zucchini, diced
1 cup green onion chopped
3/4 tsp. Cumin powder
3 tbsp chopped chives
1 lemon juiced
2 tbps. pomegranate molasses
2 tablespoons Jalapeño Pepper
1 stalk celery chopped
1/2 cup crumbled feta cheese
For the Herb Dressing
1/2 cup whole milk Greek yogurt
1 cup fresh parsley
1 cup mixed soft leafy herbs, dill, mint,
tarragon and/or cilantro
2 tablespoons chopped chives
2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice, plus ½
teaspoon zest
1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
2 teaspoons capers
1 small garlic clove
¼ teaspoon sea salt
Freshly ground black pepper
HERBY GREEN BULGUR
SALAD
In a bowl, soak the bulgur with the boiling water for 20-30 minutes,
mixing occasionally until they are soft and tender. Bulgur should not
be chewy. Set aside to cool compeletely.
Meanwhile dice and chop all your

Ingredients

.
In the bowl of a food processor combine all your ingredients for the
dressing and blend until smooth.
Add in the bulgur and mix to coat well with dressing.
In a bowl, add in all the salad ingredients with the bulgur, lemon juice
and cumin. Taste to see if you prefer to add more salt.
Drizzle with pomegranate mollases and serve right away or store in
the fridge and serve cold.
