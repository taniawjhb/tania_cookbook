GARLIC & CHILLI KALE


Ingredients


1 bag mixed kale (2 bunches, about 300 g)
2 Tbsp Extra Virgin Olive Oil
2 cloves garlic
pinch of chili flakes (peperoncino)
salt & pepper to taste


Method


1. Pull the kale leaves from their stems, then tear
leaves into pieces. Wash thoroughly.
2. Bring a large pot of water to boil. Place the leaves
in, toss, and boil for about 5-7 minutes in boiling
water until bright green and slightly softened. Drain
with a colander.
3. Heat up a large saute pan with evo oil, garlic, and
a pinch of chili flakes. Sautee for 1-2 minutes, then
add the kale leaves, adjust with salt and pepper, and
toss to coat.
4. Sautee for 5-10 minutes on medium heat, stirring
often. On e the kale is tender you are ready to serve.
5. Turn off the heat and plate.
