KOFTA -
FRAGRANT LAMB MEATBALLS


Ingredients


1/4 cup each pine nuts, almonds, and walnuts (total
3/4 cup)
1/2 cup breadcrumbs
1 small yellow onion, roughly chopped
3 cloves garlic, roughly chopped
1 small red bell pepper, roughly chopped
1 small jalapeño pepper, seeds and ribs removed,
roughly chopped
1/2 cup cilantro leaves, gently packed (okay to
substitute parsley)
900gms ground lamb
3/4 teaspoon ground cumin
Heaping 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
Heaping 1/4 teaspoon ground cardamom
Heaping 1/4 teaspoon ground cloves
1-1/2 teaspoons salt
1/4 teaspoon white pepper
Tzatziki and hummus, for serving


Method


1. Toast the nuts: Pulse nuts in a food processor
until finely chopped, then transfer to a dry skillet.
Toast over medium heat, stirring, until fragrant (5–
6 minutes). Cool in a mixing bowl.
2. Prepare the vegetables: Pulse onion, garlic,
bell pepper, jalapeño, and cilantro until finely
minced. Wring out excess liquid using a clean
dishcloth, then add to the bowl with nuts.
3. Mix the kofta: Combine lamb, cumin,
cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, salt, and white
pepper with the nut mixture. Mash with hands until
evenly blended.
4. Shape & chill: Form into patties, place on a
baking sheet, cover, and refrigerate until ready to
cook.
5. Grill: Preheat to medium-high, oil the grates,
and cook 4 minutes per side, covered, until
browned and cooked through.
6. Serve: Enjoy with tzatziki and hummus.
