PIYAZ - SHEPHERDS SALAD


Ingredients


1 can cannellini beans drained
2 tomatoes diced
1/2 red onion thinly sliced
1 cup fresh parsley chopped
1 tsp sumac
1/2 tsp Aleppo pepper 1/2 tsp salt
1 lemon juice of
2 tbsp olive oil


Method


1. Place the drained cannellini beans in a large bowl
and add chopped tomatoes, sliced red onions and
chopped parsley to the beans. Stir well and set aside.
2. In a small bowl, mix sumac, Aleppo pepper, salt,
lemon juice and olive oil and pour it on the salad.
3. Toss the salad with the dressing and refrigerate
for 30 minutes. Serve cold as a side dish.
