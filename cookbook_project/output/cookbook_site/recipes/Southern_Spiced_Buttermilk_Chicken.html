Southern Spiced Buttermilk Chicken
Crispy strips of chicken served with a fiery dipping mayo
6 large chicken breasts
2 cloves of garlic
1/2 tsp salt
2 tsp soy sauce
1/2 tsp ground black pepper
1 tsp paprika
1 tsp finely chopped fresh thyme
250ml buttermilk
6 tbsp cake flour
4 tbsp corn flour
3 eggs
300gms golden bread crumbs
Cut the chicken into equal sized strips. Now in a large bowl and all the remaining

Ingredients

for the chicken to
the buttermilk. Add the chicken strips to the buttermilk mixture and set aside for about 20-minutes
Whilst the chicken is in the marinade prepare your mayonnaise.
To prepare the fry coating, place the eggs in a flat bowl, and whisk lightly. In a second dish add the cornflour,
flour and season well with salt and pepper. In a third dish add the bread crumbs.
In a large frying pan add enough sunflower oil to shallow fry the chicken strips. Place the oil over a medium high
heat, once the oil is almost ready start to prepare your chicken strips.
Remove each strip of chicken from the marinade, and gently lay them one at a time into the flour mixture, Dust
generously on both sides and then dip into the egg wash before pressing gently into the bread crumbs. If the
strips have too much buttermilk you can remove a little with your fingers.
To test the oil is ready add a few bread crumbs, they should fizzle. Now carefully add the strips one at a time
into the hot oil, allow time for the strip to brown before turning and finishing cooking. Place the strips of some
paper towel before keeping warm in the oven. Serve with the hot dipping mayo.
