Chocolate Brownies
240g butter, melted and cooled
2 tablespoon vegetable oil
150g brown sugar
200g white sugar
4 large eggs, room temperature
2 teaspoons vanilla extract
150g all purpose flour
120g good quality, unsweetened cocoa powder
1 teaspoon salt
100g roughly chopped chocolate or large chocolate chips
100g roasted, blanched hazelnuts
Zest of 1 orange
Preheat the oven to 175°C then line a 7x11 inch baking tray with parchment paper and set aside.
In a large bowl combine melted butter, oil and both sugars. Add the eggs, vanilla, orange peel and salt then
whisk for about one minute until evenly combined and light in color.
Over the same bowl sift in the flour and cocoa powder. Gently fold the dry

Ingredients

into the wet ingredients until
JUST combined (do NOT over mix). Fold in the chocolate chunks and the hazelnuts.
Pour the batter into the prepared pan, then smooth the top.
Bake for 35-40 minutes, or until the centre of the brownies no longer jiggles and is JUST set to the touch.
Remove from the oven and allow to cool to room temperature before removing from the baking tray and slicing
into 16 brownies. Enjoy!
