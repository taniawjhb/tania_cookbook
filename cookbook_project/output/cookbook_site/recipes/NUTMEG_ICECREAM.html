NUTMEG ICECREAM

From: Island Dreams.pdf

← Back to Index | Ingredient Index

1 1⁄2 cups milk
1 1⁄2 cups heavy cream 3 large eggs
3⁄4 cup sugar
1 tablespoon grated fresh nutmeg (about 1
whole nutmeg)
1⁄8 teaspoon salt
1 teaspoon vanilla extract
In a saucepan bring the milk and the cream just to a boil. Do not
boil but turn off the heat as soon as it gets to boiling stage.
In a bowl whisk together the eggs, the sugar, the nutmeg, the salt,
and the vanilla.
Whisk 1/2 cup of the milk mixture into the egg mixture, and whisk
the mixture into the remaining milk mixture.
Cook the custard over moderate heat, stirring constantly with a
wooden spoon, until it registers 175 degrees F. on a candy
thermometer.
Transfer the custard to a metal bowl set in a larger bowl of ice and
cold water and stir it until it is cold.
Freeze the custard in an ice-cream maker according to the
manufacturer's instructions.
