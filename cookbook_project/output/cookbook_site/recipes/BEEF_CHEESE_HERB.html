BEEF, CHEESE & HERB
SAUSAGE ROLLS


Ingredients


1 small onion , peeled, whole
1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs (Note 1)
500g beef mince, fattier the better
1 egg
2 garlic cloves , minced with garlic
press or finely grated
1 beef stock cube , crumbled or 1/2 tsp
cooking salt / kosher salt
1/2 tsp salt
1/4 tsp black pepper
1/3 cup finely chopped fresh herbs,
parsely, thyme, etc
1 1/2 cups (150g) cheddar , shredded,
or other cheese of choice
1/2 cup (50g) parmesan , shredded
using box grater
2 sheets frozen puff pastry , butter is
better (24cm squares)
1 egg , separated
INSTRUCTIONS
Step 1: Preheat oven 200°C/425°F (180C fan-forced).
Step 2: Onion soak – Grate the onion in a bowl using a
box grater, reserving all the onion juices. Add panko
breadcrumbs and toss to wet.
Step 3: Then add beef, egg, garlic, stock cube, salt,
pepper and herbs. Mix well with hands to combine.
Step 4: Add cheeses, then use fingers to firstly “crumble”
the cheese through the beef before mixing well with
hands (this

Method

prevents cheese from clumping
together).
Step 5: Roll – Barely thaw puff pastry (stiffer = easier to
handle). Divide meat into 2 equal logs. Roll up in the puff
pastry, then overlap the base with double layer, sealing
with egg white. Finish with the seam side down and trim
off surplus puff pastry (about 2 cm/0.8").
Step 6: Cut each log in half. Cut 3 diagonal slashes on
the top of each sausage roll. Brush with egg yolk, sprinkle
with sesame seeds.
Step 7: Place the sausage rolls on a paper lined tray.
Bake for 35 minutes, then transfer onto rack. Bake a
further 15 minutes until pastry is deep golden and
underside is crispy.
Step 8: Cool on rack 5 minutes before attacking!
