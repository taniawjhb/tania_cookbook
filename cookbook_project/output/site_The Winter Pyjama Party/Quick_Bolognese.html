Start by adding butter and olive oil to a heavy based pot on medium/high heat
Add your finely chopped sofrito, black, (and garlic/chilli) and cook till softened and fragrant.
Next, add your meat, bay leaf and sprig of rosemary, giving it a nice stir so that flavours are evenly
distributed. At this stage, we want to brown the meat. In order to get nice caramelised meat, do not over
stir. Allow a few minutes between stirring.
Once your meat is nicely browned, add your can of tomato, tomato paste and a can of water (Use your
tomato can as it still has tomato in it!)
Reduce your heat to medium, pop the lid on and allow to simmer for 30 minutes - stirring occasionally.
