AMALFI STYLE LEMON 
CHICKEN
INGREDIENTS
6 chicken fillets 
1/2 cup all purpose flour for coating
1 tsp salt
1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
2 tbsp Lemon olive oil
2 cloves, finely chopped
Zest of 1 lemon
2 tbsp olive oil
1tsp finely chopped rosemary
1/2 cup white wine (or chicken 
stock)
1/4 cup white balsamic
1/4 cup lemon juice
1/4 cup water
Butter (optional) 
METHOD
1. Pat dry fillets with a paper towel, butterfly them, and with a meat 
mallet gently flatten to an even thickness. In a shallow dish, 
combine the flour, salt, and black pepper. Lightly dredge each 
chicken thigh in the flour mixture, shaking off any excess for a thin 
coating. 
2.  Heat oil, rosemary, garlic in a large non-stick skillet on medium-
high. Place fillet and sear 1 to 2 minutes on each side until lightly 
golden brown. Remove chicken and set aside. Deglaze pan with 
wine or chicken stock. 
3.  Add balsamic, lemon juice and lemon zest. Gently simmer for 
two minutes. Reduce heat to low, cover pan and cook for 5 minutes 
then add the chicken and cook a further 5 minutes until chicken is 
cooked through, occasionally basting. Swirl in 2 tablespoons of 
Butter olive oil for rich and glossy finish. Serve with spoonfuls of 
sauce and a drizzle of olive oil.
