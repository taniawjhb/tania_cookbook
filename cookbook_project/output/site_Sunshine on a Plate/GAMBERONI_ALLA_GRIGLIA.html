GAMBERONI ALLA GRIGLIA
1 kg of prawns
100 ml of extra virgin olive oil
4 clove of garlic, finely pasted
1 tablespoon chopped parsley
2 tsp chili powder
2 tsp paprika
60ml white wine
Salt and Pepper To Taste
Start by putting the oil in a bowl, then add the white wine, a
pinch of salt and pepper. Emulsify everything with a whisk. Then
add the pasgted garlic, paprika, chili powder and the chopped 
parsley.
Arrange the prawns in a baking dish and sprinkle them
with the emulsion you have prepared. Let it cook for about 30-40
minutes, or just the time when the embers will be ready to start
grilling them.
Place the prawns on the hot grill, then, after about 3
minutes, turn them on the other side too, to cook them evenly.
The cooking times of grilled prawns can vary depending
on the intensity of the heat and the height at which the grill is
placed. Cooking is usually very short and never lasts more than
7-8 minutes.
As soon as the prawns are evenly browned, you can
remove them from the grill and place them in the dishes or in a
tray.
