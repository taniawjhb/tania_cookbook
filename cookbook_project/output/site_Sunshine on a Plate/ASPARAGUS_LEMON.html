ASPARAGUS & LEMON

⅔ cup extra-virgin olive oil
1 large bunch asparagus, trimmed, thinly sliced
on a deep diagonal
4 garlic cloves, smashed
Zest of 1 lemon
½ tsp. crushed red pepper flakes
8 large basil leaves
1 lemon juiced
60gms. Parmesan, finely grated (about 1 cup), 
plus more for serving
Cook pasta in a large pot of boiling salted water, stirring
occasionally, until al dente. Drain pasta, reserving 1½ cups pasta
cooking liquid.
Meanwhile, heat oil in a large Dutch oven or other heavy pot over
medium-high until shimmering. Add asparagus, season with salt,
and cook, stirring often, until just beginning to take on color, about 1
minute.
Add garlic, lemon zest, and red pepper flakes and cook, stirring,
until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Add the cream and reduce by half 
.
Add lemon juice to half a cup of reserved pasta water, add this along 
with the pasta and basil to pot with asparagus mixture and return to
medium-high heat. Now add 60gms Parmesan and more  reserved 
pasta cooking liquid as needed.
Cook, tossing vigorously, but gently and adding more pasta cooking
liquid if needed, until sauce is creamy and emulsified and pasta is
coated, about 1 minute. Taste and season with more salt if needed.
Remove and discard garlic or paste and add back for a bold punchy 
flavour.
Divide pasta among bowls, placing a lemon strip in each, and top
with more Parmesan.
