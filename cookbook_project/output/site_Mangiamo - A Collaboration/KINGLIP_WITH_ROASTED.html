KINGLIP WITH ROASTED

Ingredients
500g assorted cherry tomatoes
1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
12 to 15 garlic cloves, peeled
Zest of 1 lemon
1/4 cup capers
Coarse salt
Cracked black pepper
1/2 cup loosely packed basil leaves, torn 
into halves and thirds
800gms white fleshed fish, like Kingklip 
filleted
Method
Step 1:  Preheat oven to 200C.
Step 2:  In a large roasting tray, combine tomatoes, oil, 
garlic, salt, pepper, lemon zest, capers and 1/2 the basil. 
Roast for 20 minutes. Remove from oven, gently stir. 
Return to the oven and roast until tomatoes begin to 
burst
Step 3:  Remove the tomatoes from the oven, nestle 
fish in pan with tomatoes, place back in oven and 
continue roasting 10 minutes more or until fish is no 
longer translucent and tomatoes are beginning to 
become sticky. 
Step 4:  Remove from oven. Divide fish among serving 
plates. Add remaining basil to tomatoes. Taste and 
adjust seasonings. Spoon over fish in equal portions.
