QUICK BEEF

QUICK BEEF BOLOGNESE
INGREDIENTS
600g beef mince
1 Red onion, finely chopped
1 medium sized carrot, finely 
chopped
1-2 stalks celery, finely chopped
1 clove garlic, minced 
1 small chilli, split down the centre 
but kept whole 
1 sprigs rosemary
1 bayleaf
1 can whole peeled tomato 
2 tbsp tomato paste
250ml beef stock 
500ml whole milk
Handful of fresh chopped parsley
Olive oil
Knob of butter
Salt and Pepper
METHOD
1. Start by adding butter and olive oil to a heavy based pot on 
medium/high heat
2.  Add your finely chopped sofrito, garlic and chilli and cook till 
softened and fragrant.
3.  Next, add your meat, bay leaf and sprig of rosemary, giving it a 
nice stir so that flavours are evenly distributed. At this stage, we 
want to brown the meat. In order to get nice caramelised meat, do 
not overstir. Allow a few minutes between stirring.
4.  Once your meat is nicely browned, add your can of tomato, 
tomato paste and a can of water (Use your
tomato can as it still has tomato in it!)
5.  Reduce your heat to medium, pop the lid on and allow to simmer 
for 30 minutes, after 10 minutes add your milk and continue cooking 
- stirring occasionally.  Check your seasoning, also depending on the 
strength of your chili remove it when you each a desired level of 
spiceness.
6.  Stir through fresh chopped parsley before serving with your 
favourite pasta
If your mixture starts looking too dry, add a little bit of liquid (water,
