FAGIOLINI VERDE CON

Ingredients
1 tablespoon fine sea salt 
160g fine green beans
1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
3 tbsp red wine vinegar
1 tablespoons finely sliced mint leaves
1 clove garlic, finely sliced
Freshly ground salt & black pepper
Method
1.  Bring 2 litres of water to the boil in a large saucepan, 
then stir in the fine salt
2.  Add the beans, return to the boil, cook for 3-4 
minutes until just tender.  Drain beans and plunge into 
ice water
3.  When the beans are cool, drain well and pat dry.
4.  Toss with mint, oil, vinegar, garlic, salt and pepper 
and serve at room temperature
