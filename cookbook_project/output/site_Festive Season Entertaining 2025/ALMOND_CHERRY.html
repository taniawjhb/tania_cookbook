ALMOND CHERRY

The Colony Shopping Centre, 345 Jan Smuts Avenue, Craighall, 2024.
ALMOND CHERRY 
CHOCOLATE TRIFLE
INGREDIENTS
710mls whole milk
2 tsp vanilla extract
1 strip lemon peel
6 egg yolks
150gms granulated white sugar
45gms all purpose flour
120 grams dark chocolate, chopped
Bitter Cherries
200gms fresh cherries
2tbsp sugar
90ml Amaretto
Assemble Zuppa Inglese
18 savoiardi cookies or Madeira cake
200ml cream cold - (Optional)
100gms flaked almonds, toasted till 
lightly golden.
INSTRUCTIONS
Step 1:  Place the vanilla, milk, and a strip of lemon rind 
in a medium sized pot.  Heat over low heat until the milk 
is just before simmering point. Don’t boil.
Step 2:  In a bowl whisk egg yolks, granulated white 
sugar and all purpose flour until smooth. Take care that 
there are no lumps.  Remove lemon rind from the milk.
Step 3:  Slowly pour the hot milk onto the egg yolk 
mixture, whisking constantly.  Strain the mixture back to 
the pot and place over medium low heat. Whisk 
constantly until the mixture comes to a boil and thickens. 
Simmer for 1 minute to cook the flour.
Step 4:  Add the chopped chocolate to the custard and 
stir until the chocolate has melted and is thoroughly 
combined. Cover with plastic wrap ensuring the plastic is 
in complete contact with the chocolate pastry cream
Refrigerate until thoroughly chilled.  Before using, stir until 
smooth.
Assemble Trifle
Step 1:  Cut the savoiardi in half and dip quickly but 
completely into the amaretto cherry mixture, or if using 
madeira cake slice 1cm thick and line your serving dish, 
now brush generously with the amaretto cherry  mixture.
Step 2:  Now top this with half your cherries and their 
reserved syrup.
Step 3:  Top with your chocolate pastry cream followed 
by a second layer of soaked biscuits or cake, and the 
balance of your cherries and then top with your whipped 
cream. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 
2 hours to set.
