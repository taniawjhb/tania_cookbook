CLASSIC CAESAR SALAD

For The Croutons
1/2 French Baguette, cut in half and 
thinly sliced, 0.5cm thick
3 Tbsp extra virgin olive oil
1 tsp minced garlic, 2 small cloves
2 Tbsp grated parmesan cheese
Caesar Salad Dressing:
6-8 anchovies
2 garlic cloves
2 pasteurized eggs
Juice of 1 large lemon
1 cup olive oil
½ cup grated parmesan cheese
sea salt
For the Caesar Salad:
1 large romaine lettuce, (or 2 small 
heads romaine)
1/3 cup parmesan cheese, shredded 
or shaved
Instructions
Step 1: Preheat oven to 190˚C. Cut the baguette in 
half lengthwise through the top of the baguette then 
slice diagonally into .05cm thick pieces. Place the 
breads onto a baking sheet.
Step 2: In a small bowl, combine 3 Tbsp extra virgin 
olive oil and 1 tsp of finely minced garlic. Drizzle the 
garlic oil over the croutons and sprinkle the top with 2 
Tbsp grated parmesan cheese.
Step 3: Toss until evenly coated. Spread in a single 
layer over the baking sheet and bake at 190˚C until 
light golden and crisp (10-12 minutes), or to desired 
crispness.
How to Make Caesar Salad Dressing:
Step 1: In the bowld of a small food processor add 
the anchovies and garlic and pulse 20-30 times until 
the ingredients are finely chopped.
Step 2: Next, add in the eggs and ½ of the lemon 
juice and process on high for 20 seconds until 
completely mixed together and smooth.
Step 3: While processing at high speed, slowly drizzle 
in the oil until it becomes thick and creamy.
Step 4: Finish by adding in the cheese, remaining ½ 
lemon juice, and salt, and process on low speed until 
combined.
Refrigerate until ready to use.How to Make Caesar 
Salad:
Step 1: Rinse, dry and chop or tear the romaine into 
bite-sized pieces. Place in a large serving bowl and 
sprinkle generously with shredded parmesan cheese 
and cooled croutons. Drizzle with caesar dressing and 
toss gently until lettuce is evenly coated.
